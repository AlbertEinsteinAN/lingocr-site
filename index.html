Lingocr

Lingocr is a personal study app for reading games, comics and books in another language, Japanese first. It reads the text on the screen, shows a dictionary card for each word, and keeps track of the words being learned, with flashcards.

It runs on its user's own computer and Android phone. The study data is backed up to the user's own Google Drive, through which the phone and the computer also exchange their changes.
